The core idea

Your board pays you

Everything in NexaFlow comes down to one thing: your board. Understand the board and you understand the whole system.

When you join a stage you get a board. A board is the set of positions sitting underneath you. Every time one of those positions gets filled by somebody joining, you get paid.

You are paid the same fixed amount for every position, no matter who fills it or how it got filled. The payment lands in your wallet in the same transaction that person joins. There is nothing to claim and nothing waiting in a balance somewhere.

A Stage 1 board. Two positions directly under you, four under them. The arrows show the direction money travels: upward, to you.

The key point. You are not paid only for the two people directly under you. You are paid for every position on your board, including the ones further down.

Reading the table

Take Stage 2. You pay $60 to enter. Your board has 14 positions and each one pays you $10. If the whole board fills you have received $140.

You do not have to wait for a full board to start earning. Each position pays the moment it is filled. A board that is half full has paid you half.

Why the fees rise. A bigger entry fee funds a bigger payment per position. The board size stays the same at 14, so the higher stages simply move more money through the same structure.

Filling your board

Three ways a position fills

Your board does not only fill with people you personally introduce. This is the part most people find surprising, and it is the part that makes the system work.

1

You introduce someone

They join and take a position on your board. Straightforward.

2

Spillover from above

Someone above you introduces a person, but their own two slots are already taken. That person moves down and lands on your board instead. You are paid for them, even though you have never met them.

3

Growth from below

Somebody on your board introduces a person of their own. That person still sits inside your board, so you are paid for them too.

Spillover. The dotted line is a new member being placed further down because the positions above are taken.

Spillover and spillunder are the same event. Looked at from above it is spillover, because the person spilled out of a full board. Looked at from below it is spillunder, because they arrived under you without you doing anything. Same person, same payment, two names.

Rollover, also called turnover

A full board starts again

Filling your board is not the end. It resets and you earn from it again.

When the last position on your board is filled, the board rolls over. Your rollover counter goes up by one, the positions clear, and a fresh empty board opens underneath you. People start filling it, and you start being paid again at the same rate.

This can happen over and over. There is no cap on how many times a board can roll over.

One rollover cycle. Gold positions are filled and paid. The dotted board on the right is the new empty one.

What rollovers are worth

Each completed board pays you the full board amount for that stage. Five rollovers at Stage 2 means five full boards.

Being honest about this. These figures assume boards actually fill. A board fills when people keep joining. If activity slows, boards fill slowly or stop filling, and earnings slow with them. Nobody can promise you a full board.

What you can rely on

The rate is fixed and public. Stage 2 pays $10 per position to whoever owns the board, every time, for everyone. It is written into the contract and it is the same for every member. What varies is how fast positions fill, not what you are paid for them.

Awards

Rollover milestones

On Stage 2 and above, every five rollovers unlocks an award on top of your normal board earnings. Awards repeat. Ten rollovers unlocks two, fifteen unlocks three, and so on. Surplus carries forward, so nothing is lost by passing a milestone before it is paid.

Stage 1 has no award. It is the smallest board option.

Awards are capped against your own earnings. An award can never exceed a set share of what your board actually generated. This is deliberate. It stops anyone gaming the milestone by filling their own board with wallets they control, because doing that costs them more than the award returns.
Fairness

The rules are the same for everyone

Fixed rates

Every position on a Stage 2 board pays $10. Not more for early members, not less for late ones.

Paid immediately

Money reaches your wallet in the same transaction. The system never holds your earnings.

Placement is automatic

Where a new member lands is decided by the contract, not by an administrator deciding who gets a good spot.

Nothing hidden

Fees, board sizes and payment rates are public and checkable on chain by anyone.

Before you join

What to understand

A clear explanation of the risks matters more than an optimistic one. Read this part properly.

1

Your earnings depend on other people joining

Positions on your board are filled by new members. If new members stop arriving, your board stops filling and your earnings stop with it. This is the single biggest thing to understand.

2

A full board is not guaranteed

Every figure in this document describes a board that fills completely. Yours may fill slowly, partly, or not at all.

3

Entry fees are not refundable

Once you pay to enter a stage, that fee has been distributed. There is no withdrawal of your entry.

4

Later stages cost meaningfully more

Stage 6 costs $4,860 to enter. Only commit what you can afford to lose.

This document is an explanation, not advice. It describes how the mechanism works. It is not a prediction of what you will earn and it is not financial advice. Make your own decision.
